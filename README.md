# AI Workflow Automation Portfolio

Welcome to my AI Workflow Automation portfolio.

This repository documents my journey towards becoming an **AI Operations Specialist** and **AI Automation Developer** by solving real business problems with AI.

Rather than building isolated demos, each project is designed to automate a genuine business process using AI, APIs, and workflow automation tools.

## Objective

The goal of this portfolio is to demonstrate my ability to:

* Analyse business processes
* Design AI-powered workflow automations
* Integrate multiple applications and APIs
* Improve AI-generated workflows rather than simply accepting them
* Test, document, and explain automation solutions clearly

## My Approach

I use AI as a development partner—not as a replacement for understanding.

Every workflow follows the same process:

1. Understand the business process.
2. Generate an initial solution with AI.
3. Review every decision.
4. Improve the workflow.
5. Test edge cases.
6. Document the solution.
7. Explain it clearly.

## Why This Portfolio Exists

Businesses don't just need people who can use AI—they need people who can apply AI to solve real operational problems.

This portfolio is my record of developing those practical skills through hands-on projects that focus on automation, reliability, scalability, and business value.
